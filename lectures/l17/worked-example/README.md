# L17 worked example, held back

Moved out of Lecture 17 on 2026-10-28, to be studied and taught in a later session. Nothing in
this folder is published: it is not in `_toc.yml`, and CI renders only `lectures/*/slides.md`.

- `l17-tiny-llm.ipynb`: train a tiny GPT (842,881 parameters) on the notes of Lectures 1 to 16,
  then test it on one key term per lecture. Generated by `build_notebook.py`; executed with its
  outputs by `../figures/make_figures.py tiny`, which also draws the L17 training figures from the
  same run.
- `slides.md`: the five code slides that went with it (one transformer block, the whole model,
  inputs and targets, the training loop) and the demo marker. It renders on its own:
  `npx @marp-team/marp-cli lectures/l17/worked-example/slides.md --html -o /tmp/we.html`, with
  `_static/widgets.js` beside it for nothing more than the theme.

To bring it back: add `- file: lectures/lNN/...` for the notebook under that session's notes in
`_toc.yml`, and move these slides into that session's deck.
