## Course assessment

- **Weekly assignments:** one per teaching module, released one week and due the next.
- **Mini-project:** launched at Lecture 13 and due at the end of the week of October 26. It no
  longer has dedicated class days, and it takes the place of the weekly assignment for its
  module.
- **Final project:** proposed by November 4, built through the first half of November, and
  presented on November 18 and 23. The repository, recorded demo, and written report are due
  before your presentation slot.
- **Open sessions:** November 30 and December 2 carry no new content and are held in reserve.
