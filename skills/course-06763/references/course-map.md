## Projects

- **Miniproject**: unsupervised fault detection on the Tennessee Eastman Process, PCA
  (T² and SPE) against a forecast-residual detector. Team project.
- **Final project**: student-chosen, teams of four, over the last weeks of term. Proposal,
  7-minute presentation, short written document, and data plus code.

Read both from the site for requirements and dates; do not summarize requirements from here.

## Finding a topic

1. `https://kitchingroup.cheme.cmu.edu/f26-06763/genindex.html`, the general index. Each entry
   links to the section that defines the term, and includes clusters for `case study`,
   `failure mode` and `metric`.
2. The table above, to narrow down which lecture to fetch.
3. A text search over the fetched `_sources/lectures/lNN/notes.md` files.
